  - a refutation that the held-out probe belongs to the training corpus;
  - a refutation that its authorized view equals the training view;
  - a unique commitment preventing corpus or probe substitution;
  - parent and learned traces over exactly the same held-out view;
  - exact consumption of the learned prediction by the proposal;
