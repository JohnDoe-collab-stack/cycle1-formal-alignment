Dependency boundary
-------------------

`SegmentedResidualRole` remains the abstract residual kernel.
`AbstractSegmentedTurning` consumes that kernel and remains independent of
circular presentations. `StrongPerimetralTurning` is the circular/perimetral
instance of that abstract turning layer.

`Alignment.Constitutive`, `Alignment.FinitePersistence`, and
`Alignment.ReadoutPersistence` remain generic persistence/alignment theory.
`StrongPerimetralTurning.ConstitutivePersistence` is the explicit integration
point between the circular instance and `Alignment.Constitutive`.

`RepresentationBoundary.DiagonalizationKernel` imports only `Init` and is
independent of alignment theory.

`RepresentationBoundary.CircularStatusRepresentation` imports
`StrongPerimetralTurning` and the diagonal kernel. It is a separate application
to already established proposition-level circular statuses.

No `Alignment/*` or `StrongPerimetralTurning/*Persistence` source imports
`RepresentationBoundary`. Therefore naturality, finite persistence, mediated
coherence, and mediated pasting do not depend on the diagonal
representation-boundary result.

Representation-boundary naming
------------------------------

The diagonal branch uses representation vocabulary matching the proved content:

  GlobalRepresentationClosure
  noGlobalRepresentationClosure
  ExactCircularStatusRepresentation
  localExactRepresentation_hasDiagonalExit
  localExactRepresentation_notGloballyClosed
  diagonalRepresentationExit
  canonicalUnitCircularStatusRepresentation

The central interpretation is:

  local exact representation of selected statuses
  +
  a constructively produced diagonal status outside internal representation
  ->
  failure of global representation closure

This is not presented as a second stage of alignment.

Mediated coherence
------------------

The branch contains the isolated generic kernel:

  MediatedTransitionCoherence.lean

and its finite-alignment specialization:

  Alignment/MediatedTransitionCoherence.lean
  Alignment/MediatedTransitionPasting.lean
  Tests/MediatedTransitionCoherenceRegression.lean
  Tests/MediatedTransitionPastingRegression.lean

These modules formalize observed commutation, faithful/local-reflection upgrade,
identity, sequential composition, and pasting. Intermediate faithfulness is not
required for observed pasting; terminal reflection suffices for literal outer
commutation.
